# Global AI Builders Hackathon 2026

## Description
Welcome to the Global AI Builders Hackathon 2026 — a 48-hour sprint where developers, designers, and visionaries come together to build cutting-edge AI-powered applications. Whether you're into healthcare, fintech, education, or open innovation, this is your stage to ship something that matters.

## Problem Statement
Build an AI-powered solution that addresses a real-world challenge in one of the following domains: Healthcare, Education, Financial Inclusion, or Climate Action. Your solution must leverage a large language model, computer vision pipeline, or autonomous agent framework. Judges will evaluate on innovation, technical depth, and real-world impact.

## Rounds

Round 1: Idea Submission — Online
- Teams submit a 500-word project description and problem framing
- Duration: 3 days
- Shortlisting based on innovation and feasibility

Round 2: Build Sprint — Hybrid
- 48-hour intensive hackathon; teams present a working prototype
- Duration: 48 hours
- Technical mentors available throughout

Round 3: Final Presentations — In-Person
- Top 10 teams pitch to a panel of industry judges
- Duration: 5 minutes pitch + 3 minutes Q&A
- Judging criteria: Impact, Execution, Innovation
